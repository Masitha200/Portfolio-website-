Aether Academic

Premium Study Scheduler & Focus Hub

A premium, glassmorphism-styled study management platform designed for high-achieving students. Features a smart weekly/monthly study scheduler, interactive task kanban board, configurable Pomodoro focus timer with ambient soundscapes, and a comprehensive analytics dashboard — all running 100% offline in your browser with localStorage persistence.

Smart Weekly & Monthly Scheduler

Plan study sessions with customized color-coded subjects, start times, durations, and completion tracking across a beautiful calendar grid.

Pomodoro Focus Engine

Configurable 25/15/45-minute focus sessions with ambient soundscapes (rain, library hum, lofi beats) and dark-mode focus states for deep work.

Task Kanban Board

Sort assignments and workloads by urgency (To Do, In Progress, Completed, Overdue) with priority labels, due dates, and quick-action controls.

Analytics Dashboard

Track total study hours, completed tasks, scheduled sessions, productivity ranking, and subject focus breakdowns with visual bar charts.
